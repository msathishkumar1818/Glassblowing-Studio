LEVEL 01 • NO EXPERIENCE NEEDED

THE NOVICE HALF-DAY IMMERSION

Our signature introductory workshop designed for curious newcomers, couples, and creative enthusiasts.

Step inside the hot shop and experience the fundamentals of traditional glassblowing in a guided, hands-on session. No previous studio experience is required, and every participant receives individual instruction throughout the process.

From your first introduction to the furnace through shaping, coloring, and finishing, this half-day experience gives you a focused introduction to the rhythm, heat, tools, and creative possibilities of working with molten glass.

Expect an active, collaborative studio experience rather than a traditional classroom. You will stand at the bench, work alongside your instructor, observe demonstrations up close, and participate in each stage of creating your pieces.

The atmosphere is energetic and warm, with the glow of the furnace, movement of tools, and transformation of glass creating an experience that is as visual as it is hands-on.

Beginners are encouraged to experiment while respecting the material and the studio process. Your instructor will adjust the level of guidance to help you understand the technique without taking away the feeling of making something yourself.

DAY IN THE HOT SHOP

CHRONOLOGY OF A WORKSHOP DAY

09:00 AM
Thermal Physics & Hot Shop Safety Briefing

Understanding radiant heat zones, emergency shutoffs, Kevlar sleeve usage, and blowpipe hygiene.

10:15 AM
The First Gather & Marver Practice

Taking the pipe into the 1,150°C pot to gather an optic punty and shaping on the cast iron slab.

11:45 AM
Inflating the Hollow Bubble & Color Pick-up

Rolling hot glass over crushed mineral frit, reheating in the glory hole, and blowing the initial cylinder.

01:00 PM
Gaffer Bench Tooling & Lehr Annealing

Constricting the neck with jacks, shearing the top, and placing your completed piece into the 510°C annealing oven.

ZERO-COMPROMISE SAFETY

REQUIRED HOT SHOP ATTIRE & PROTOCOL

MANDATORY ATTIRE

100% Natural Cotton Fibers

Synthetic fabrics (polyester, nylon, fleece) melt near radiant heat. Wear 100% cotton jeans and a long-sleeve cotton shirt.

FOOTWEAR

Closed-Toe Leather Boots

No sandals, canvas sneakers, or mesh trainers. Heavy leather boots or sturdy work shoes protect against falling hot shears.

STUDIO PROVIDED

Kevlar Sleeves & Eyewear

We provide ANSI-approved didymium flare safety glasses, Kevlar forearm sleeves, and heat-resistant split leather gloves.
