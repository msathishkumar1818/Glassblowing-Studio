METHODOLOGY

THE 6-STAGE BESPOKE LIFECYCLE

Rigorous milestones ensuring structural integrity, color fidelity, and seamless rigging.

1
Spatial Dialogue & Site Analysis

Reviewing ceiling load ratings, natural sun angles, and ambient illumination spectrums.

2
Scale Watercolor & 3D CAD Modeling

Rendering precise 1:1 scale canopy maps and individual element suspensions.

3
Crucible Color Swatch Melts

Melting small test batches of custom mineral oxides to verify color harmony under client lighting.

4
Hot Shop Production & Hand Tooling

Master Gaffer Elena Vance gathers and tools each individual element at the furnace.

5
72-Hour Annealing & Lapidary Cold Cut

Stress relief cooling in computer lehrs followed by diamond-lapidary rim grinding.

6
White-Glove Delivery & Rigging

Our studio technicians assemble the canopy, connect LED drivers, and hand-hang all elements.

CUSTOM FINISHES

METALLIC FOILS & SURFACE TREATMENTS

METALLIC INCLUSION

Neodymium Violet

Applied directly to the second hot gather and torched at 1,000°C to create micro-fissures and ethereal lavender flecks within clear crystal.

COLD-WORK FINISH

Satin Acid Etching

Hand-carved shallow wheel scallops (*battuto*) that scatter ambient light into a soft velvet luminescence.

OPTIC MOLDING

Venetian Ribbed Prisms

Plunged into 24-point steel star molds while molten, producing sharp vertical facets that cast dramatic shadows across nearby walls.
