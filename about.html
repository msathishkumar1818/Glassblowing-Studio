CRAFT PHILOSOPHY

THE THREE SACRED FORCES

Glassmaking is an honest dialogue between human intuition and raw physics. Three elemental disciplines guide every creation.

PILLAR 01

MOLTEN ALCHEMY

We melt pure silica sand and custom mineral oxides at 2,100°F. Our signature lavender and deep mauve formulas produce rare optical depth and internal refraction.

Continuous Kilns 2,100°F
PILLAR 02

KINETIC BREATH

No industrial molds. Each silhouette is coaxed entirely by hand rotation, lung pressure, and gravity. Every piece captures the gaffer's vital breath in liquid crystal.

Mold-Free Freeform 100% Intuitive
PILLAR 03

LAPIDARY FINISH

After 18-hour annealing in digital lehrs, each piece undergoes diamond-wheel beveling, pumice stone smoothing, and cerium oxide polishing for museum brilliance.

Cold Lapidary Cut Optical Grade
