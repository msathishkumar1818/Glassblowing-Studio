KNOWLEDGE BASE

COMMONLY ASKED QUESTIONS

None at all. Our Novice Half-Day Immersion is created specifically for beginners. You will be guided 1-on-1 by a resident gaffer at every single step, from furnace gather to the final pipe tap.
Hot glass must spend 24 to 48 hours cooling in the annealing lehr to prevent thermal stress fractures. Pieces are ready for studio pickup starting Tuesday following your weekend class, or we can ship them worldwide.
All commissioned glass is packed into custom laser-formed foam inside heavy Baltic birch crates with impact and tilt sensors. Our studio technicians travel on-site globally to personally supervise installation and uncrating.
