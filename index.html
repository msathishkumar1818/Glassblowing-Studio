CHOREOGRAPHY OF SECONDS

THE 6 SACRED RITES OF HOT GLASS

A glassblower has fewer than ninety seconds of malleable work time before the crystal stiffens and must return to the 1,150°C glory hole.

01

The Crucible Gather

Stainless blowpipe enters the 1,150°C pot to gather glowing molten crystal.

Time: 00:00 1,150°C
02

The Steel Marver

Rolling the hot gather across cast iron to center mass and form a thermal skin.

Time: 00:30 980°C
03

The First Breath

A gentle breath pulse expands the initial hollow core bubble.

Time: 01:10 920°C
04

Cherrywood Jacks

Fruitwood blocks and steel jacks sculpt the walls and constrict the neck.

Time: 02:45 850°C
05

The Pontil Transfer

Punty iron transfer frees the lip for opening, trimming, and flaring.

Time: 04:15 800°C
06

48h Lehr Annealing

48-hour computer-governed cooling relieves all internal stress.

Time: 48 Hours 510°C → 20°C
